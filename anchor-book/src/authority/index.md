# Authority

Authority establishes why an anchor's property must hold. An anchor cites at
least one requirement or decision:

- [Requirements](requirements.md) state required outcomes and constraints,
  adapted from Symposium's tenets and design.
- [Decisions](decisions.md) record choices that govern the system, adapted from
  its design and its accepted and completed RFDs.

Each record states its own status: `accepted` records are part of the agreed goal
design, and `draft` records are still under review. The engine validates
references between records; review establishes whether an authority justifies
the property that cites it.
